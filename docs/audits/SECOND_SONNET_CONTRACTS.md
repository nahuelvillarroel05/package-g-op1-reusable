## Required distinctions

* Assertion extraction, source-declared attribution and logical comparison are
  separate operations. English clause rules are bounded and conservative; they
  do not establish general semantic accuracy or authenticate an agent.
* Active contradictions must be machine-visible. A correction can supersede only
  a comparable predicate, identity and scope; the individual history remains.
* Nested content discovery is not agent attribution. Excluded content must retain
  source/path/hash/reason and unknown attribution without becoming a confirmed claim.
* Source bytes, committed descriptor bindings and the normalized result
  determine rederivation. Missing new-contract derivation fields must fail explicitly.
  Historical reading can establish byte integrity with derivation not verified and
  semantic validation not proven; it cannot supply new semantic approval.
* Logical support, byte integrity, derivation and evaluator authentication are
  independent qualifiers. A source declaring EVALUATOR_REPORT is not authenticated.
* Review metadata records declared identity and execution guards. A model name,
  review identifier and commit are not cryptographic provider attestation or proof
  of independent custody. A real review must have observable invocation evidence.

Public test fixtures are synthetic development inputs. No reserved holdout or
private GOLD is used. Full Linux validation and the third external Sonnet review
remain separate work. See the third-review handoff for exact candidate references.
