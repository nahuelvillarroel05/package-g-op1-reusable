# Prospective engineering and publication organization

The Owner designated `experiment` as the single experimental public branch. `main` remains unchanged. RUN-001, RUN-002 and RUN-003 retain their original execution and release identities; new publication records are retrospective representations and do not rewrite historical metrics. Their original public directories remain referenced, without copying large historical ledgers.

RUN-004 retains all 10,096 selected memberships and 80,768 unit-predicate pairs. Authentic archived outputs, task definitions, evaluator records and PGOP1 audit results are distinct evidence types. Missing outputs, unresolved associations, incompatible versions and unknown provenance cannot be converted into successful semantic results. Existing reserved evidence remains excluded from preflight qualification.

The new receipt helper validates a literal prospective rev004 receipt representation and observes synchronous callback timing. It does not make CampaignV2's compact checkpoint representation literally equivalent to rev004, integrate a complete final receipt publication path, enforce arbitrary callback process memory limits, or approve a normative amendment. Supported development processing is limited to controlled inert evidence producers; arbitrary callbacks and external task execution are outside the qualified scope.

Full selected processing rights, compatible output coverage, predicate and abstention qualification, exposure review, checkpoint representation and exact Owner execution bindings remain technical gates. Development test/preflight results are engineering evidence only. Public source bodies and original trajectories with unresolved redistribution rights remain withheld; legitimate aggregate results and commitments may be published under the publication policy.

Remote publication requires an authorized successful push followed by fresh commit, tree and file-byte verification. Branch cleanup additionally requires public historical reachability, local recoverable Git preservation, independent review, and fresh unchanged HEAD/PR/protection/default checks. A failed authentication attempt leaves the local candidate and all historical remote branches intact.
