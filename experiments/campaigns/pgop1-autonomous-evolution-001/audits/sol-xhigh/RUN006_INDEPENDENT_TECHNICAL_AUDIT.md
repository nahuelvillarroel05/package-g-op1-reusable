## Findings and prospective corrections

### F01 — Incorrect missing-output reason on bound outputs (moderate reporting defect)

All 20 terminal and Engine own-result payloads have `authentic_output_present=true`, `audit_disposition=AUDITED` and `missing_evidence=[]`, yet six `NOT_EVALUATED` predicate rows per unit (120 total) state `reason="No authentic output is bound"`. The frozen implementation seeds every slot from `definition_disposition` in `runtime/candidate/run_framework/predicates.py:69-83`, then sets output presence at `:243-252` and overwrites only the two BFCL-mapped slots at `:259-278`. This explains the observed contradiction. It does not change the 20 auditor statuses or the mapped predicate decisions, but makes the trace explanation factually false.

**Reproducer proposal:** On a synthetic bound BFCL unit, call `build_audit_payload` with a fixed auditor result and assert every unmapped `NOT_EVALUATED` row has a scope-specific reason that does not deny the bound output, while all eight decisions, applicability values, abstention classes and result status remain unchanged. Also keep a no-output fixture asserting the original missing-output reason. A prospective RUN007 correction may change explanation text only; it must not promote `NOT_EVALUATED` to `SUPPORTED` or add a semantic predicate.

### F02 — Four planned batches of five were not enforced as execution boundaries (moderate contract/evidence gap)

The frozen outer configuration states `planned_batches=4` and `units_per_batch=5`; inner `resource_budget.max_units_per_shard=5`. `core.py:173-185` can produce a shard plan, but the actual `run005.py:273-285` executes all dataset units in one loop and never calls that planner or records a batch boundary. The receipts prove ordered processing and one-unit checkpoints, and the total 20-unit budget held; they do **not** prove that execution stopped at a five-unit batch boundary before beginning the next five. The generic planner in `planning.py:7-14,17-55` sorts IDs lexically; using that plan directly for dispatch would reorder the frozen numeric prefix (`_10` before `_2`). This is an execution-control gap, not an observed resource overrun or a reason to rewrite RUN006.

**Reproducer proposal:** In a synthetic fresh attempt, use a recording caller with the frozen 20-unit order and max batch five. Require an immutable batch-start record, at most five calls per batch, a verified terminal/checkpoint and immutable batch-terminal record before the next batch's first callback. Inject a material failure and verify an open start with no next-batch dispatch. Keep one campaign-level resource budget and `checkpoint_interval=1`; recovery remains read-only with no auto-resume. Describe these as execution batches unless the sorted shard planner's contract is explicitly versioned. `SAFE_TO_SIMPLIFY` is inapplicable to the needed boundary; change requires domain review and focused tests.
